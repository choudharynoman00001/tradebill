Why send a written estimate

A written estimate sets the price, the scope and the timeline before work begins. It avoids arguments later and shows the client you are organized.

What to include

  • Clear description of the work
  • Itemized materials and labor
  • Total price and how long the quote is valid
  • Deposit required to start
