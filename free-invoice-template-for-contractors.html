Free invoice template for contractors

Make a clean contractor invoice in two minutes. Add your logo, line items, deposit and due date, then print or save as PDF.

What a contractor invoice needs

  • Your business name and contact details
  • Client name and job address
  • Invoice number and date
  • Itemized labor and materials with quantity and rate
  • Deposit paid, balance due and due date
  • Payment instructions

Tips to get paid faster

Send the invoice the same day the job ends. Set a due date of 7 to 14 days. State late fees clearly. Keep a numbered record of every invoice so you can find it later.
